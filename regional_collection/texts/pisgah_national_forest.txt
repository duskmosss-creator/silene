# Pisgah National Forest Wilderness & Trail Guide

## Key Wilderness Areas
1. **Linville Gorge Wilderness ("The Grand Canyon of the East")**
   - Elevation Range: 2,000 ft to 4,120 ft (Shortoff Mountain, Table Rock, Hawksbill).
   - Terrain: Extremely rugged, steep terrain with unmaintained trail corridors.
   - Key Routes: Linville Gorge Trail (39 miles), Babel Tower Trail, Chimneys Gap.
2. **Looking Glass Rock & Davidson River Area**
   - Massive granite pluton rising 3,969 ft. Popular for technical rock climbing and steep day hikes (6.5 miles round trip, 1,700 ft gain).
3. **Cradle of Forestry in America**
   - Historic site of the Biltmore Forest School founded by Dr. Carl A. Schenck in 1898. birthplace of scientific forestry in North America.
