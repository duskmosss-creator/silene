# Shenandoah National Park & Skyline Drive Guide

## Key Highlights
1. **Skyline Drive (105 Miles)**
   - Traverses the crest of the Blue Ridge Mountains in Virginia from Front Royal to Waynesboro.
   - 75 scenic overlooks providing vistas of the Shenandoah Valley and Piedmont.
2. **Old Rag Mountain Trail**
   - Most famous and challenging rock scramble in Shenandoah (9.4 miles round trip, 2,415 ft gain).
   - Requires narrow fissure maneuvers and false-summit scrambles.
3. **Appalachian Trail in Shenandoah (101 Miles)**
   - Gentle, well-graded trail section frequently intersecting Skyline Drive.
