# Blue Ridge Parkway Crest & Highest Peaks

## Key High Points
1. **Mount Mitchell (6,684 ft)**
   - Highest peak in North America east of the Mississippi River.
   - Located at Parkway Milepost 355.4 in the Black Mountains range.
   - Features balsam fir spruce forest summit trail.
2. **Grandfather Mountain & Mile High Swinging Bridge**
   - Milepost 305. Rugged terrain with backcountry ladder trails (Grandfather Trail).
3. **Craggy Gardens & Pinnacle**
   - Milepost 364. Famous for vast rhododendron blooms in June.
