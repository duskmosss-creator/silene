# Nantahala National Forest & Whitewater Wilderness

## Key Features & Trails
1. **Nantahala Gorge & River Corridor**
   - World-famous class II-III whitewater kayaking & rafting corridor.
   - Legend of Uw'tsûñ'ta (Cherokee legend of the great serpent).
2. **Joyce Kilmer Memorial Forest**
   - One of the last remaining virgin old-growth forests in Eastern America.
   - Features 400-year-old Poplar trees with trunks exceeding 20 feet in circumference.
   - Joyce Kilmer Loop Trail: 2.0-mile easy loop through ancient hemlocks and poplars.
3. **Whiteside Mountain**
   - Highest sheer cliffs in Eastern North America (over 750 ft vertical drop).
   - Critical nesting habitat for peregrine falcons.
